Ask AI helps users understand VMECC pages, common workflows, uploaded guidance, and what to check before they continue.

Ask AI responses are advisory only. Users should verify important details in the actual record, workflow status, assigned module, and approved operational guidance before acting on a response.

Ask AI cannot submit records, approve workflows, change permissions, or replace administrator decisions.
